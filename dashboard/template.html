Sector Pulse: where the jobs are and where the money went

Five years of payroll jobs, job postings and stock-market returns by sector, for California and the whole United States, plus a hand-collected look at the last three months. Which industries are hiring, which are shrinking, which ones investors rewarded, and what that means for someone looking for an entry-level analyst role.

    Data. Payroll jobs: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Current Employment Statistics (national, state and metro), via FRED. Stock sectors: month-end adjusted prices of the SPDR Select Sector funds and SPY, from Yahoo Finance. Last three months: BLS JOLTS and the Challenger, Gray & Christmas job-cut reports, typed in by hand and checked against the published totals. Job postings: Indeed Hiring Lab job postings index (seasonally adjusted, 7-day average, Feb 1 2020 = 100). An index measures the trend in postings, not the number of open jobs.

    Method. Python (pandas) cleans the files and loads them into SQLite; the analysis is written in SQL (CTEs, window functions). Code, queries and results: github.com/resulisik/california-jobs-by-sector.
